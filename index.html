Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about building, comparing live retailer prices, and selecting compatible PC parts.

How does Rigwise check component compatibility?
Rigwise cross-verifies processor sockets (e.g. AM4, LGA1700) with matching motherboards, checks memory generation (DDR4 vs DDR5), validates display outputs (APU vs dedicated GPU), and calculates real-time power draw against power supply ratings with a safe headroom margin.
Where do the live prices come from?
Our pricing service aggregates real-time product listings and shopping search results across premier Indian retailers such as MDComputers and Amazon. In addition, we maintain a baseline catalog estimate so you can design builds even offline.
What budget should I target for gaming in 2026?
For solid 1080p competitive esports gaming (CS2, Valorant, GTA V), ₹40,000–₹50,000 is a sweet spot. For AAA titles at high 1080p/1440p settings, target ₹70,000–₹1,00,000. Enthusiast 1440p/4K rigs typically range from ₹1,20,000 to ₹1,80,000+.
Can I export or print my quotation to buy locally?
Yes! You can copy a formatted specification sheet to your clipboard or print a clean quotation with a single click in both the Custom Builder and the Shop Demo Cart. Take it directly to Nehru Place, Lamington Road, SP Road, or order online.
